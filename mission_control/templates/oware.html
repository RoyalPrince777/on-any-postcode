Abapa rules
  1. Pick a highlighted house on your row. Sow one seed in each successive house counterclockwise. Skip the starting house if you make a full lap.
  2. If the last seed leaves exactly 2 or 3 in an opponent’s house, capture those seeds. Work backward through adjacent opponent houses with 2 or 3. If that would empty their row, all the seeds stay on the board.
  3. If your opponent has no seeds, you must feed their row. If no legal move exists, each side collects the seeds still on its own row.
  4. Capture 25 or more to win. A final score of 24–24 is a draw.
  5. OAP automatically ends a loop when the same board and turn appear three times within 120 positions without a capture. Each side collects its remaining seeds.

Nam-Nam rules
  1. Sow counterclockwise from a house you own. If the last seed lands in an occupied house without a capture, pick up its seeds and keep sowing. Stop on an empty house or a last-seed capture.
  2. When sowing brings a house to four, its owner captures those seeds. If it was the last seed in your hand, you capture the four.
  3. Feed an empty opponent territory if possible. If feeding is impossible, the owner of the remaining seeds collects them. The player who captures the next set when eight seeds remain also gets the last four.
  4. Use captured seeds to refill houses in groups of four. Extra groups win houses across the board. Capture all territory to win the match.
  5. OAP replays a round with the same territory if a relay loops or exceeds 256 pickups, or the same board and turn appear three times within 120 positions without a capture.
