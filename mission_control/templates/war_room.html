Seven review lenses

Every major scenario is challenged seven ways

① Purpose & Human Benefit ❤️

Why does this exist and who genuinely benefits?

② Truth & Evidence 🔎

Is the information real, current, sourced and sufficiently verified?

③ Safety & Guardian 🛡️

What privacy, safety, youth, humanitarian or abuse boundary could fail?

④ Architecture & Alignment 🧠

Is it correctly placed, coherent and aligned without duplication or drift?

⑤ Operations & Resilience ⚙️

Dependencies, performance, degradation, recovery and reversibility.

⑥ Value & Sustainability 💷

Created Value, cost, revenue, resources and sustainable operation.

⑦ Authority & Accountability 👑

Who approves, who executes, what is recorded and how is it audited?
