OAP Ludo rules
  1. Two to four players, four pieces each. Player 1 starts. Turns and pieces move clockwise.
  2. Roll to enter on 6 or move a piece already on the track by the full roll. A 6 earns another roll.
  3. Land on opponents to send their pieces back to base and roll again. Stars and starting squares are safe. Pieces can share squares, and stacks do not block moves.
  4. If no move is possible, pass the turn unless you rolled a 6. There is no limit on consecutive sixes.
  5. After one lap, use your own home lane. An exact roll gets a piece home. The first player to bring all four home wins.
