Use first

Core decision intelligence

These are the lenses the War Room should reach first. They are ordered to reduce unsupported conclusions and premature deployment.

🔎 Truth Intelligence

What is actually proven, observed, inferred, untested, unknown or contradicted?

Output: truth state + confidence.Run evidence check

🧾 Evidence Intelligence

What evidence supports each important claim, when was it collected and how strong is it?

Output: claim → evidence → source → time → confidence → result.Open evidence

🧩 Gap Intelligence

What is missing, unfinished, disconnected, duplicated, placeholder, hard-coded or unproven?

Output: actionable gap map.Open acceptance gaps

📊 SWOT Intelligence

What are the strengths, weaknesses, opportunities and threats around this system or decision?

Output: strategic position, not final authority.Run SWOT

⚠️ Risk Intelligence

What can fail, how likely is it, how large is the blast radius and what reduces the risk?

Output: probability × impact × exposure with mitigation and fallback.Guardian / risk check

🔗 Dependency Intelligence

What relies on this component, and what downstream systems change if it moves or fails?

Output: upstream and downstream dependency map.

◎ Alignment Intelligence

Does the proposal fit OAP architecture, naming, Human Authority, privacy, local-first and no-fake-green rules?

Output: aligned, conditional or conflicting.Check alignment

🧪 Scenario Intelligence

What happens under alternative conditions, failures, load, outages or conflicting choices?

Output: bounded what-if outcomes; no secret execution.Open next scenario

🚦 Readiness Intelligence

Is code, testing, security, runtime, data, rollback, monitoring and certification really ready?

Output: category-by-category readiness, blockers kept visible.Open production proof

🧭 Decision Intelligence

Given the evidence, risks, gaps, scenarios and constraints, what is the best bounded next action?

Output: recommendation + alternative + reason + confidence + required approval.Ask SMI

👑 Judgement Intelligence

Should the recommendation pass the governed gate, pass with conditions, hold or be rejected?

Judgement recommends; Human Authority remains final where approval is required.Open Judgement

🟣 Learning Intelligence

What did reality teach us after the decision, and where did prediction differ from outcome?

Output: lesson → HRM receipt → improved future reasoning.

Cognitive loop

How the lenses work together

The system should not fire every lens at once. It moves through a bounded reasoning loop and only escalates when the problem requires it.

01 ObserveTruth → Evidence → Data
02 UnderstandGap → Dependency → Architecture → Alignment
03 AnalyseSWOT → Risk → Security → Privacy → Performance → UX
04 ExploreScenario → Impact → Opportunity → Trend
05 DecidePriority → Readiness → Decision → Judgement
06 AuthoriseConsequential action stays governed; Human Authority remains final.
07 LearnOutcome → HRM receipt → lesson → future reasoning.

OAP truth lights

Signals describe evidence, not optimism

🟢 ProvenOperational and supported by sufficient current evidence.
🟡 IncompleteFunctional or promising, but material proof or work remains.
🟠 Material issueA meaningful blocker, weakness or risk needs work.
🔴 FailedBroken, unsafe or contradicted by evidence.
🔒 Governed lockIntentionally unavailable until the correct authority or proof gate passes.
🟣 LearningObservation, evaluation or learning is in progress; it is not proof of readiness.

Green rule: Truth Intelligence + Evidence Intelligence must support the claim before 🟢 is awarded.
