## Evidence and authority gates
Every heritage assertion intended for publication should carry: claim, source identifier/link, source type (founder statement, oral testimony, primary record, secondary research), observed/publication date if known, consent/publication rights, review state, and explicit uncertainty/conflicting claims. Unknown fields remain unknown, not inferred. Founder-approved seed is not historical proof; timestamp of a generated response is not source publication time.

Royal lineage, traditional offices, succession, and personal family records require appropriate human/cultural review. No AI decides who holds a traditional office. Founder Final governs OAP actions, not Ghanaian customary institutions. Private family data stays private by default; consent and applicable law govern disclosure.
