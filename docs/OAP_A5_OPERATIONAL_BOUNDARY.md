# OAP A5-Ready Operational Boundary

Status: LOCKED
Owner: ON ANY POSTCODE
Authority: Founder / Human Authority

## Purpose

A5-ready means the UI and War Room can prepare for deeper autonomy without enabling uncontrolled autonomy.

A5 is not allowed to become an independent ruler of OAP.

## A5 may run

A5-ready systems may run these governed, reversible, proof-bound functions:

1. Read-only proof review across OAP World, SMI, Kernel, Guardian, HRM, Green Gate, OAP Atlas, Movement and OAP Direct.
2. Signal aggregation across the 21 OAP core signals.
3. Gap detection and prioritisation.
4. Route Matrix scoring from captured evidence.
5. Private Guard scoring from captured fail-closed evidence.
6. OAP Atlas source-health scoring from source, timestamp and stale-data evidence.
7. Movement schema readiness review.
8. Direct supplier/listing/inventory readiness review.
9. Pictures and lifecycle readiness review.
10. HRM receipt completeness review.
11. War Room scenario preparation.
12. Draft action packs for Founder approval.
13. Generate safe public briefs with secrets/private details removed.
14. Recommend KEEP / UPGRADE / MERGE / REMOVE.
15. Prepare rollback checklists.
16. Prepare deploy checklists.
17. Prepare database migration checklists.
18. Prepare supplier certification checklists.
19. Prepare incident review notes.
20. Prepare youth/safety/compliance review notes.
21. Prepare next-build instructions for Builder after approval.

## A5 may not run

A5 must not:

- self-approve
- bypass Founder authority
- bypass Guardian or Aegis
- deploy code
- migrate production databases
- capture payment
- move SIKA as money
- dispatch real-world people or vehicles
- confirm reservations without supplier proof
- collect hidden tracking
- expose private media
- claim external marketplace authority
- make emergency-service authority claims
- publish public claims without proof
- change its own permissions
- unlock A5 by itself

## War Room operational meaning

Operational War Room means:

- it can gather evidence
- it can score proof lanes
- it can show route/button/action state
- it can prepare command packs
- it can record required receipt fields
- it can show what is blocked
- it can pass approved work to Builder only after Founder approval

Operational does not mean uncontrolled execution.

## Final law

A5 prepares.
Guardian protects.
Green Gate proves.
HRM remembers.
Kernel coordinates.
Founder decides.
Builder executes only after approval.
