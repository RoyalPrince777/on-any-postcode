# The Spot front-door upgrade

The Spot now presents a clearer postcode-first local front door without adding a signup wall or moving protected actions into public space.

Visitors can see their supplied postcode context, jump to the existing local-data lookup, and understand at a glance whether a priority surface is for browsing, local data, a protected space, or a gated action. The compact dashboard keeps all existing Spot capabilities available under More in The Spot.

The labels are deliberately narrow: a public card being browsable does not claim checkout, dispatch, payment, live tracking, verification, or another protected transaction is operational.

No internal provider details, secrets, infrastructure configuration, or private readiness metadata are exposed by this presentation layer.
