## Clean naming rule

SMI is the intelligence.
A1 to A7 are operating levels.

A-levels are not agents, not products, not separate brains and not separate systems.

## Level ladder

| Level | Name | Meaning | State |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A1 | Manual support | Human-led notes, drafts and simple assistance | Legacy / supported |
| A2 | Guided assistance | SMI can organise information and suggest next steps | Supported |
| A3 | Bounded tool support | SMI can use approved tools inside narrow limits | Compatibility |
| A4 | Supervised autonomy | SMI can run bounded workflows, review proof and propose actions | Live governed level |
| A5 | Governed operational preparation | SMI can coordinate War Room evidence, 21-signal scoring, gap detection and command packs | Locked / ready boundary |
| A6 | Governed operational execution | SMI may execute limited approved operations only after proof, Guardian pass, Green Gate pass, HRM receipts, rollback and explicit Human Authority | Future locked |
| A7 | Certified organism-scale autonomy | SMI may coordinate organism-scale operations only after all A6 gates, external audit, legal/compliance proof, observability and emergency halt proof | Constitutional locked |
