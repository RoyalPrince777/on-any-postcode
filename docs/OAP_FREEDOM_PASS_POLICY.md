## Principles

Born Local. Built Global. Earth Is Our Turf.
One Race, Human Race.

- Free public community access remains available without purchase, compulsory payment card, coercive follows, or doom-scrolling.
- No monthly subscriptions, recurring card charges, automatic renewal, hidden debit mandate or forced top-up.
- A person chooses each payment explicitly and sees the full price, currency, entitlement, exclusions and terms before approving.
- Do not sell people's data, infer personal payment capacity, penalise users for not paying, use undisclosed paid ranking or alter safety decisions for money.
- Preserve one canonical My Card identity and the existing OAP Pass access layer: do not create duplicate cards, profiles, wallets, accounts, checkout systems or entitlement authorities.
- SIKA remains separate from access passes and is not asserted to be bank money.

## Four optional access choices

1. **Free World** — no charge to join OAP, create the one My Card or use defined basic community/discovery features; no card required.
2. **OAP Year Pass** — single authorised payment for a clearly stated 12-month entitlement. Does not auto-renew. Access returns to Free World at expiry without deleting My Card or owned content.
3. **OAP Forever Pass** — one-time charge for an explicitly scoped entitlement for the supported lifetime of that service. Never advertise unrestricted perpetual infrastructure, unlimited storage, third-party costs or all future products. Publish continuity, change and cessation terms before purchase.
4. **Tap & Go** — one-off per-use purchase with price and permission confirmed each time; no rolling billing or surprise debit.

These are **product choices**, not active charges or proven software. Prices and currencies are undecided. Any proposed local pricing must be transparent, non-discriminatory and consistent with law; avoid profiling or hidden exchange mark-ups.

## Fail-closed implementation and acceptance gates

- **Identity/access**: authenticated identity binding, scope-limited pass checks, no duplicate entitlements, no automatic privileged access.
- **Pricing**: transparent quote and currency; explicit confirmation before payment, no saved-card requirement; compliant records, applicable consumer cancellation/refund terms, accessible disclosures.
- **Lifecycle**: Year starts and expires reliably; no auto-renew; Forever only provides promised scope; Tap & Go delivers exactly the paid item; failures and refunds reconcile without double charging.
- **Safety**: no user data sale, youth protections, accessibility, no dark patterns, no paid safety overrides, no private content leakage.
- **Operational**: supplier fulfilment, invoicing/tax treatment, chargebacks, consumer support and disputes, entitlement reconciliation, durable audit.
- **Regulatory**: payment acceptance through a legally authorised arrangement; review payment, consumer, privacy and cross-border obligations before charging; no unlicensed banking or money-transmission claim.
- **Release**: independent Guardian and HRM evidence, exact-head tests, real checkout/entitlement proof, live production proof and Founder Final approval.
