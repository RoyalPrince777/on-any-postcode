## No demo rule

OAP must not present placeholder surfaces, mock status cards, decorative modules, fake green checks, fake live labels, fake data, fake users, fake payments, fake distribution, fake routing, fake AI actions, or fake integrations as finished production capability.

A module is green only when it has:

1. A real owner system.
2. A real route or service surface.
3. A real data source, record store, or explicitly read-only approved source.
4. A public/private boundary.
5. Guardian-safe failure behaviour.
6. HRM/audit record where the action matters.
7. Tests or live checks proving the claim.
8. No misleading legal, financial, music, data, safety, routing, or platform claim.

If any of these are missing, the module must say BUILDING, LOCKED, or NEEDS APPROVAL instead of LIVE.

## No missing gaps rule

Every OAP product area must show one of four states:

- LIVE: implemented and checked.
- BUILDING: present but incomplete.
- LOCKED: intentionally unavailable until authority, compliance, data, provider, safety, or approval exists.
- REMOVED: deliberately removed, with no duplicate replacement unless approved.

No hidden amber gap should be called green.
