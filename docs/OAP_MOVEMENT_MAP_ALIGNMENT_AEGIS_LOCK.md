## 1. Purpose

Movement Intelligence and Map Intelligence help OAP understand where things are, where they are going, what routes are safe to show, and what signals matter by postcode, borough, county/region, country, continent and global layer.

They are not surveillance systems, military systems, policing systems, or uncontrolled dispatch systems.

## 3. Mandatory Alignment check

Every Movement or Map feature must pass Alignment before green:

1. Human-first purpose
2. Local-first usefulness
3. Public/private boundary
4. No third-party authority over OAP
5. No fake live route or location claim
6. No secret or private location exposure
7. No unsafe public panic signal
8. No policing, targeting, or military use
9. HRM receipt required for important actions
10. Founder / Human Authority final for consequential actions

## 4. Mandatory Aegis / Guardian check

Aegis is the protection shield around Movement and Map Intelligence.

Aegis must block:

- covert tracking
- stalking assistance
- private home exposure
- real-time personal location leaks
- doxxing
- military targeting
- vigilante coordination
- unsafe crowd movement instructions
- emergency claims without source proof
- fake road closures or fake evacuation instructions
- dispatching people without Human Authority approval

Aegis allows:

- public-safe area context
- postcode and borough level discovery
- official-source crisis map signals
- route planning after safety checks
- sports venue/event movement planning
- accessibility-aware movement context
- OAP-owned open map intelligence using approved public data

12. UI does not overclaim

## 10. OAP maps policy

Production maps should prefer OAP-owned/open-data/open-infrastructure routes.

Allowed as governed sources:

- OpenStreetMap-style public map data
- official public transport/disruption feeds where allowed
- official weather / emergency feeds
- official humanitarian feeds
- OAP first-party records
- approved community-submitted events after moderation

Blocked until proof:

- Google dependency as default authority
- hidden location tracking
- private-address public map pins without consent
- unlicensed map-data claims
- untested live route claims
- emergency instruction claims without official source

## 11. War Room gate

War Room must review Movement / Map features when the feature affects:

- real-world travel
- youth movement
- public crisis wording
- emergency response
- event crowd flows
- private location data
- personal safety
- public claims
- spend / booking / dispatch

Flow:

Founder request → Movement/Map Intelligence → Alignment check → Aegis/Guardian check → HRM proof check → War Room challenge → Human Authority decision → deploy/publish/lock.
