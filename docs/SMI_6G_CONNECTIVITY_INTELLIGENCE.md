# SMI 6G Connectivity Intelligence

6G Intelligence is a bounded specialist capability under:

Technology Intelligence → Connectivity Intelligence → 6G Intelligence

It is not a new Intelligence world, brain, telecom operator, radio controller, or execution authority.

## Runtime truth boundary

- 6G architecture model: ready
- production 6G network: not claimed
- autonomous eSIM provisioning: disabled
- autonomous radio control: disabled
- network execution authority: disabled
- Human Authority: final

The capability may research, reason about, compare and plan future connectivity, edge intelligence, integrated sensing, mobility, device-to-device links, mesh/local networks, satellite connectivity and resilient fallback paths. Real network provisioning or transmission remains outside this advisory capability unless separately implemented, legally permitted, explicitly approved and runtime-proven.

## SMI Organiser boundary

The Founder-only `/mission/organiser` surface can display verified schedule
mirror receipts and import a reviewed connectivity-brief summary with
primary-source links. Imports are append-only audited drafts. They do not store
the external automation prompt or raw task output, approve an architecture
change, or grant schedule, network, radio or deployment execution authority.

An external scheduled task is not part of the first-party SMI record until its
timing metadata has a verified Organiser receipt. A delivered research brief is
not part of the first-party evidence record until the Founder imports a bounded
summary. Automatic external-task synchronization remains unclaimed.

The Founder-only
`/api/smi-organiser/connectivity-briefs/<brief_id>` GET/PUT contract is the
machine-readable first-party intake boundary. It requires the existing OAP
identity, Founder authority and CSRF protections, and writes through the same
append-only audited draft store. The contract is not an external transport: no
scheduled sender is connected until separately configured and proven.
