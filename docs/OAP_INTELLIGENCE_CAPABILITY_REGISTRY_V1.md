# OAP Intelligence Capability Registry v1

## Constitutional boundary

SMI remains one brain with exactly seven Intelligence Worlds:

1. Earth Intelligence
2. Language Intelligence
3. Life Intelligence
4. Movement Intelligence
5. Civic Intelligence
6. Civilisation Intelligence
7. Matrix Intelligence

The reusable Intelligence Capability Registry does not create additional Worlds, brains or agents. Capabilities are made available to one or more of the seven Worlds according to need.

Nexus connects capability context. Specialist Matrix Intelligence agents remain inside Matrix. Oasis provides environment/presentation. Guardian protects permissions, privacy and safety. Human Authority remains final.

## Truth boundary

Registry software readiness does not mean supply or transaction readiness.

The following remain fail-closed until real integrations, tests, legal/compliance gates and production evidence exist:

- accommodation, transport and activity inventory connections
- live availability and pricing feeds
- booking creation/modification/cancellation
- payment processing and settlement
- pass/ticket issuance
- commission settlement
- provider/supplier contracts
- regulated or licensed activity

No hidden fees are permitted by the registry policy. External providers and suppliers never gain OAP authority. Human Authority remains final for consequential execution.
