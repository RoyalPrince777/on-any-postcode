# OAP Hard Locks Authority Board

Status: LOCKED  
Owner: Human Founder Authority  
Applies to: OAP World, SMI, Living Kernel, Guardian/Aegis, HRM, Green Gate, OAP Atlas, Movement Intelligence, OAP Direct and all public/private surfaces.

This board reinforces non-negotiable hard locks. These items are not product tasks, sales features, hidden capabilities or autonomy goals. They remain blocked unless a future lawful, reviewed, Founder-approved and proof-backed governance process explicitly creates a safe boundary.

## Hard locks

| Lock | State | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A5 autonomy | LOCKED | SMI may not self-promote, self-approve, deploy independently, spend, migrate, dispatch, publish claims, or override Human Authority. |
| Payment capture | LOCKED | OAP World, OAP Direct, SIKA and connected surfaces must not capture payment until compliance, provider, ledger, refund, receipt and approval proof exists. |
| Real-world dispatch | LOCKED | No person, vehicle, supplier, field action, rescue, delivery or physical response can be dispatched without approved human workflow and safety proof. |
| Confirmed reservation claim without supplier proof | LOCKED | Quotes, holds and requests may exist, but public confirmation is blocked until authorised supplier confirmation and receipt proof exist. |
| Hidden tracking | BLOCKED | Covert tracking, hidden location collection, silent Live Spot, biometric location inference and non-consensual movement monitoring are blocked. |
| Private media leak | BLOCKED | Private media, Founder assets, supplier/private photos, receipts and admin-state media must never leak to public routes. |
| External marketplace authority | BLOCKED | External platforms cannot become OAP authority. They may only be reviewed sources or future bounded handoff routes with proof. |
| Emergency authority claim | BLOCKED | OAP must not claim emergency-service authority, official crisis command authority, rescue authority or public safety mandate without lawful proof. |
| Public claims without proof | BLOCKED | Public surfaces must not claim live, certified, confirmed, official, guaranteed or operational status without evidence. |

## Green Gate rule

Green Gate must keep a lock red/locked when evidence is missing, stale, unverifiable or outside approved authority.

## Guardian rule

Guardian/Aegis must block any workflow that attempts to bypass these locks by renaming the action, hiding it inside another feature, routing it through an external provider, or presenting a simulated output as live proof.

## Kernel rule

The Living Kernel may coordinate review and recommend safe next steps. It may not approve, execute or bypass these locks.

## HRM rule

HRM may record evidence, receipts and decisions. HRM records do not themselves create authority; they preserve proof for review.

## Founder authority

Human Founder Authority remains final. Even Founder approval must still respect safety, legality, privacy, compliance and evidence requirements.
