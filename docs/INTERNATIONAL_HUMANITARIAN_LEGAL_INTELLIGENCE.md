# International Humanitarian Intelligence — Legal Intelligence v1

## Canonical placement

International Humanitarian Intelligence is a cross-system civilian specialist capability inside the single SMI brain. Humanitarian Legal Intelligence is one child of that umbrella. It is not a new Intelligence World, government, court, law firm, sanctions authority, military capability, or autonomous execution authority.

## Legal/protection stack

Humanitarian Legal Intelligence organises review across:

1. International Humanitarian Law (IHL)
2. International Human Rights Law
3. Refugee, asylum, displacement and statelessness law
4. International Criminal Law
5. Disaster Law and International Disaster Response Law (IDRL)
6. Law of the Land — current applicable domestic law
7. Customary International Law
8. Traditional and customary domestic law where legally recognised
9. Child humanitarian protection law
10. Accessibility and disability-rights law
11. Humanitarian health law and medical protection
12. Humanitarian data and privacy law
13. Digital humanitarian protection law
14. Cultural heritage protection law
15. Humanitarian environmental protection law
16. Sanctions, counterterrorism and humanitarian exemptions
17. Humanitarian principles: humanity, neutrality, impartiality and independence

## Primary source families

The architecture is designed to prefer current primary/authoritative material from:

- ICRC and the Geneva Conventions / IHL resources, including customary IHL, national implementation and humanitarian data protection.
- United Nations human-rights treaty resources for human rights, children and disability protection.
- UNHCR for refugee, asylum, displacement and statelessness protection context.
- IFRC Disaster Law / IDRL for disaster preparedness, response and international disaster assistance.
- UN Security Council materials for sanctions regimes and humanitarian exemptions.
- UNESCO for the 1954 Hague cultural-property framework and related instruments.
- Official domestic legislation, courts and regulators for the actual law of the land.

Source families are not a substitute for checking treaty status, reservations, implementing legislation, emergency measures, sanctions and current case/regulatory law in the real jurisdiction.

## Law of the Land

A country is mandatory before the resolver can prepare a domestic-law review envelope. Region/local context may further narrow the law. The current runtime does not claim a complete live global legislation, treaty-status or sanctions database.

## OAP Law of the Jungle

OAP Law of the Jungle is an internal ethics and resilience code only. It is never statutory law, international law, customary international law, or a basis for overriding applicable domestic or international law.

## Humanitarian data protection

The architecture grades humanitarian data from Level 0 Public to Level 4 Life-Critical. Precise civilian locations, safe-house information, survivor/witness locations and family-reunification locations are Level 4 and are not public by default.

## Truth and authority boundary

The software may identify potentially applicable legal frameworks, protection issues, evidence requirements and escalation needs. It must not claim to:

- issue final jurisdiction-specific legal advice;
- authoritatively classify an armed conflict;
- decide whether a war crime, crime against humanity or genocide occurred;
- clear a sanctions transaction;
- replace competent courts, regulators, emergency authorities or qualified legal counsel;
- treat internal OAP principles as law;
- perform military targeting, surveillance, weapons support or offensive cyber activity.

Human Authority remains final for OAP decisions, while external legal/regulatory authority remains with the competent real-world institutions.
