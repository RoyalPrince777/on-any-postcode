# The Spot — Visitor Contract

The Spot is ON ANY POSTCODE's public postcode-first local front door.

## Public law

- Browse first. Forms appear only after a visitor deliberately opens an action.
- No account or password is required to browse The Spot.
- My World and other private actions remain protected and fail closed.
- A postcode supplied in the URL is display context only. It does not prove device location, identity, residence, or presence.
- Local weather and hierarchy must be resolved through the existing bounded location experience before they are presented as local data.
- Visitor-facing state labels describe only the surface being opened: Browse, Local data, Protected, or Action gated.
- Browse never means checkout, payment, dispatch, live tracking, verification, or another protected transaction is operational.
- The Spot must not expose internal providers, secrets, operational blockers, infrastructure configuration, or private readiness metadata.
- Existing Spot capabilities remain available; compact presentation must not delete product functions.

## Product hierarchy

OAP World → The Spot → The Link → LinkUp.

Spatial context remains postcode-first while connecting upward through Borough / District → County / Region → Country → Continent → Global → Universe.
