## Independent accountability review contract
For each future submitted claim or proposed mission task, record:
- `case_id`, `review_timestamp`, `requester`, `purpose`, `scope` and `source_links`
- source reliability and provenance; clear separation of fact, allegation, inference and opinion
- at least one opportunity for evidence-based counterview/dissent; retain minority objections and unresolved questions
- privacy/data-minimisation and child-safety considerations; no unauthorised personal data extraction
- relevant law, human rights, impartial treatment and protection against improper political manipulation
- conflict-of-interest declaration and named human reviewer
- proposed action, intended permissions, explicit forbidden actions and rollback path
- Guardian/Green Gate check, HRM receipt reference, and Founder Final decision **for activation**, if activation is later requested

Record status vocabulary: `draft`, `needs_evidence`, `reviewed_with_dissent`, `founder_scope_approved`, `held`, `rejected`. None mean `active` or `green`.
