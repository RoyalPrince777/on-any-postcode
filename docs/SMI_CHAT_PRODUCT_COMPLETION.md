# SMI Chat product completion

This change consolidates the visible private Founder chat identity around **Sovereign Megaverse Intelligence (SMI)** without creating another chat or Studio engine.

## Canonical ownership

- SMI Chat is the private Founder front door.
- SMI AUTO is the visible orchestration mode.
- `smi_canonical_controller.js` remains the single submit, microphone, voice, stop, camera and screen-capture owner.
- OAP Studio Intelligence remains the canonical media intelligence engine.
- HRM/JOOG remains the continuity and audited-memory layer.
- War Room remains the governed research, challenge and review layer.
- Guardian and Human Authority remain unchanged.

## Product truth

The UI may say **SMI**, **Sovereign Megaverse Intelligence**, and **SMI AUTO**. It must not claim continuous live screen/video understanding when only a bounded captured frame is supported.

A source-level passing test is not a substitute for live handset proof. Microphone, camera and screen permissions require supported-device runtime verification before those interactions are called production-proven.

## Scope lock

This completion slice does not change Founder authentication, passwords, permissions, database schema, execution authority, publishing authority or payment authority.
