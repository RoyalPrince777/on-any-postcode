# OAP Travel — Direct Supply Policy

## Locked model

OAP Travel uses one public catalogue supply lane:

`Certified OAP Supplier → OAP Listing → OAP Listing Photos → Availability → Pricing → OAP Travel → OAP Booking → Reservation`

Only suppliers directly onboarded and Certified by OAP may enter the public catalogue.

## External data boundary

External marketplace information may be fetched on demand for research, comparison or discovery context. It is not Partner Supply, is not imported into the OAP public catalogue, does not become OAP inventory, and cannot create an OAP reservation or payment authority.

OAP must preserve source provenance when external data is discussed and must never imply that an external marketplace is an OAP partner unless a separate real commercial relationship is explicitly approved in future.

## Listing pictures

OAP Direct listing pictures are first-party OAP records. A listing owner must confirm that they own each picture or are authorised to use it. JPEG, PNG and WebP are accepted within bounded file-size/count limits. Third-party marketplace photography must not be copied into an OAP listing without permission.

## Truth boundary

No fake supplier, listing, availability, price, capacity, photograph, reservation, payment, Pass or commission state may be used to make readiness appear green. Human Authority remains final for consequential actions.
