Status: LOCKED
Owner: Human Authority / Founder
Scope: Sovereign Megaverse Intelligence, Command Center, War Room, ISAC, Safe Signals, Sports Intelligence, HRM, Guardian, Connected Systems, Function Health, Green Gate Board.

## Purpose

This record locks the full signal registry for ON ANY POSTCODE.

Signals are not decoration. Signals are evidence inputs that tell the Command Center what is live, what is building, what is stale, what is blocked, and what needs Human Authority approval.

No signal may turn a dashboard green unless it has proof.

## Signal law

Every signal must carry:

1. name
2. source
3. timestamp
4. freshness window
5. trust level
6. public/private classification
7. Guardian safety status
8. HRM/audit destination where important
9. Green Gate result
10. safe next action

If any required field is missing, the signal is not green. It is BUILDING, STALE, UNVERIFIED, LOCKED, or NEEDS APPROVAL.

## Master signal states

- LIVE: current, connected, checked, safe and proven.
- BUILDING: route or data exists but proof is incomplete.
- STALE: source has not refreshed inside its allowed freshness window.
- UNVERIFIED: data exists but source/proof is weak or missing.
- LOCKED: intentionally unavailable until proof, compliance, licence, permission, or safety gate exists.
- BLOCKED: Guardian stopped the signal for safety, privacy, legal, youth, public-claim, or sovereignty reasons.
- NEEDS APPROVAL: Human Authority must decide before action.
- CERTIFIED: test passed and evidence was recorded.

## Signal freshness windows

Suggested default windows:

- Render deploy/log signals: 5 to 15 minutes for live release gates.
- Health endpoint signals: 1 to 5 minutes.
- Crisis/disaster signals: 5 to 30 minutes depending on source and severity.
- Weather alerts: 15 to 60 minutes.
- Sports live scores: 30 seconds to 5 minutes depending on feed.
- Fixtures and league tables: 5 to 60 minutes.
- HRM decision/approval records: permanent until superseded.
- Architecture locks: permanent until a newer certified record replaces them.

## Green Gate rule

A signal turns green only when:

1. route exists
2. source exists
3. source timestamp exists
4. access rule works
5. data is fresh enough
6. failure mode is safe
7. Guardian passes or blocks safely
8. HRM records important action
9. dashboard button is working, locked, or blocked truthfully
10. test/check proves the claim

If not, the signal must not be green.
