# OAP Safe Signals v0.3

## Purpose

OAP Safe Signals helps people understand the world from their postcode outward and points them toward lawful, peaceful, verified, human-first action.

It is civic education and world-signal intelligence. It is **not** political control, an election replacement, legal advice, financial advice, autonomous campaigning, a petition platform that fabricates signatures, or an authority that replaces government or official services.

## Canonical public promise

> OAP shares verified facts, official links, weather signals, community needs, and lawful civic education. OAP does not provide legal or financial advice, does not fake signatures, does not pressure people, and does not replace official services.

## Geography

Postcode → Borough / City → Country → Continent → World → Universe.

The Universe layer is educational: astronomy, space weather, satellites, Earth observation, navigation/GPS and long-term science learning. It does not support fantasy or authority claims.

## Better World Mentorship loop

1. **Observe** — identify the signal.
2. **Verify** — source, date, place, freshness, corrections and uncertainty.
3. **Explain** — what happened, who is affected, why it matters, what is known and unknown.
4. **Protect** — youth, privacy, vulnerable people, addresses, panic, hate and targeting.
5. **Connect** — official and trusted lawful channels.
6. **Record** — source, date, risk, impact, action, decision, correction and outcome.
7. **Learn** — HRM may learn from audited outcomes; it never autonomously executes civic action.

## v0.3 public boards

- `/signals` — World Signals.
- `/weather` — Weather Signals. Weather is preparation, not fear.
- `/news-facts` — verified/developing factual news with sources and corrections.
- `/civic-voice` — official lawful participation links; every OAP item is non-binding.
- `/mentorship` — youth-safe, adult, business and Community Captain guidance.

Weather and News Facts use the shared `oap_world_signals` evidence model in v0.3 to avoid unnecessary duplicate schemas.

## Risk labels

- **GREEN** — verified/useful/calm/practical.
- **AMBER** — developing or needs additional verification.
- **RED** — high-risk material requiring review. Red records are never automatically published.

## Civic Voice law

OAP may explain official petitions, consultations, registration and public-participation channels. OAP must not fabricate signatures, automate pressure, misrepresent OAP sentiment as a legal vote, harass officials, harvest private data, or recruit youth into political campaigns.

Community polls added later must be explicitly non-binding sentiment only.

## Debt education law

OAP may teach debt literacy, fair restructuring, anti-predatory lending, transparency, community wealth, small-business resilience and routes to qualified support. It must not advise debt evasion, ignoring contracts/courts, or present itself as legal/financial professional advice.

## Humanitarian boundary

Safe Signals reuses OAP's existing civilian-first humanitarian laws: protect life, preserve dignity, verify truth, support civilians and safeguard youth. No military targeting, tactical detail, named-person tracking, vigilante coordination or precise civilian-location exposure.

## Youth boundary

Youth mentorship is education and protection only. No adult political pressure, debt pressure, crisis fear, graphic exploitation or campaign recruitment. Youth-safe material focuses on community help, weather safety, anti-bullying, nature, culture, sport, learning and family support.

## Governance

Guardian protects safety/privacy. Governance/Chancellor review applies to sensitive civic/privacy questions. Archivist/source logic preserves evidence and corrections. Challenger/War Room may stress-test risks. HRM records outcomes. **Human Authority remains final.**

No Safe Signals component creates another SMI brain, Intelligence World or autonomous authority.
