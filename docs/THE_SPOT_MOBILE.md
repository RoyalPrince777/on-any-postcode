# The Spot mobile acceptance

The Spot is mobile-first because it is the local front door.

Acceptance requirements:

- Priority cards reflow without horizontal scrolling.
- Postcode context and its action remain readable on narrow screens.
- Tap targets remain distinct and keyboard-focusable.
- The front door remains browse-first and does not become a long form.
- More in The Spot keeps secondary capabilities discoverable without overwhelming the first screen.
- The Link, OAP World, and My World remain clearly separated.
- No device-location permission is requested by the front-door presentation itself.

Visual/runtime acceptance still requires a deployed mobile check; this document is not proof that it has passed.
