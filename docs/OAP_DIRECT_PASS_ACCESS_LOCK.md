## Card roles

### OAP Pass

Purpose: access, entry, holds, reservations, trips, venues, events, offers and member privileges.

Rules:

- The pass can represent access eligibility.
- The pass can connect a member to OAP Direct listings, offers and journeys.
- The pass can surface access status such as active, pending, suspended, expired or locked.
- The pass must not bypass supplier confirmation, payment compliance, identity checks, safety checks or Founder gates.
- The pass must not expose private location, supplier, payment or HRM data publicly.

### SIKA Digital Card

Purpose: value, rewards, SIKA, SEEDS, credits, community benefit records and future payment-ready boundaries.

Rules:

- SIKA can record rewards and value signals.
- SIKA must not claim bank, deposit, regulated money, captured payment or withdrawal capability without approved compliance evidence.
- Payment capture remains locked until provider, legal, refund, receipt and Green Gate proof exist.

### My Card

Purpose: identity, profile, certification, member status, Founder/member boundary and access relationship.

Rules:

- My Card owns identity state.
- OAP Pass can read permitted access status from My Card.
- Direct Intelligence must not expose private identity data to public listing pages.
