# The Observation Ladder 777 — SMI alignment

Status: Founder-approved conceptual architecture; this document does not assert runtime implementation or grant new access or authority.

The ladder has **seven observation/intelligence levels**. **Founder Final / Human Governance is above and outside the count**, never an autonomous intelligence stage.

| Level | Meaning | Reuse / boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 01 Individual | A human experiences and observes. | Human-supplied authorised inputs only; do not infer blanket consent. |
| 02 Collective Humanity | People share knowledge and observations. | Existing OAP community pathways and human contributions; preserve access controls. |
| 03 Digital Memory | Preserve relevant information. | Reuse canonical SMI memory and HRM/JOOG governed receipts; no raw private chain-of-thought, credentials, or unrelated private data. |
| 04 Artificial Intelligence | Analyse permitted information and relationships. | Reuse existing SMI AUTO/specialist tools; no duplicate engine. |
| 05 Reflective Intelligence | Assess outputs, uncertainty and limitations. | Reuse bounded `oap/smi/autonomy.py` observation, memory and coherence review. Self-review is not independent certification. |
| 06 Planetary Intelligence | Correlate permitted cross-environment, community and global observations. | Scope to authorised, attributable, privacy-safe aggregates; no claim of universal live access or omniscience. |
| 07 Independent Oversight — The Eye That Watches The Eye | Challenge the observer, inspect evidence and flag misuse. | A separate reviewer/audit boundary; preserve dissent and audit evidence. Do not equate SMI self-review with independence or grant reviewer execution authority. |

## Human Governance — above all seven

Founder Final remains the consequential-action decision boundary, with Guardian and Green Gate. Votes and observational outputs inform decisions but grant no authority. Approval is not execution. The canonical bounded autonomy engine explicitly blocks independent approval, self-application, deploy, permission change, payment capture and other consequential actions.

## Seven intelligence families

The ladder is the observation hierarchy. The families below are reusable intelligence lenses, not separate engines and not extra ladder levels.

| Family | Includes |
| --- | --- |
| Truth | truth, evidence, provenance, uncertainty |
| Understanding | analytical, causal, contextual, comparative |
| Systems | dependency, architecture, temporal, planetary |
| Foresight | predictive, scenario, trend, opportunity |
| Protection | security, privacy, risk, boundary, resilience |
| Adaptation | learning, recovery, reflection, improvement |
| Governance | audit, ethics, compliance, human review |

These seven families may operate across all seven ladder levels. The architecture is therefore **7 ladder levels × 7 intelligence families**, but remains one governed SMI rather than 49 separate engines.

## Cross-cutting intelligence lenses

Keep all of the following as reusable lenses inside the seven families:

- **Truth Intelligence** — distinguish observed, proven, inferred, predicted, conflicting, unavailable and unknown.
- **Evidence Intelligence** — preserve source, time, permission, freshness, evidence, confidence and limitations.
- **Causal Intelligence** — separate correlation from supported causal explanation.
- **Systems Intelligence** — understand dependencies, architecture and multi-component effects.
- **Context Intelligence** — interpret place, time, intent, prior state, environment, permissions and dependencies.
- **Temporal Intelligence** — distinguish historical evidence, current state, trend and future projection.
- **Predictive Intelligence** — express scenarios, assumptions, confidence and failure conditions without presenting forecasts as facts.
- **Risk Intelligence** — examine operational, privacy, security, financial, legal, reputational, dependency and irreversible-action risk.
- **Scenario Intelligence** — compare best, expected, stress, failure and recovery cases where useful.
- **Adaptive Intelligence** — change recommendations when evidence changes without silently changing authority or permissions.
- **Emotional Intelligence** — recognise emotional context without claiming machine emotion or using vulnerability for manipulation.
- **Social Intelligence** — understand roles, collaboration, disagreement and social context without undisclosed behavioural profiling.
- **Cultural Intelligence** — preserve cultural, linguistic, jurisdictional and community context rather than assuming one universal norm.
- **Comparative Intelligence** — compare options against explicit common criteria.
- **Quantitative Intelligence** — use measurements, probabilities, statistics and thresholds with units and uncertainty.
- **Qualitative Intelligence** — preserve narrative, meaning, lived context and non-numeric evidence.
- **Gap Intelligence** — identify current state, required state and the smallest real missing evidence or capability.
- **Recovery Intelligence** — detect, isolate, rollback, restore, verify and learn from failures.
- **Learning Intelligence** — convert verified outcomes into governed lessons; unverified assumptions do not become durable truth.
- **Adversarial Intelligence** — challenge stale evidence, hidden assumptions, weak dependencies, overconfidence, irreversibility and false Green.
- **Privacy Intelligence** — enforce purpose, permission, minimum necessary data, bounded processing, retention and deletion.
- **Boundary Intelligence** — distinguish analyse/recommend/observe authority from approve/execute/change-permission authority.
- **Ethical Intelligence** — examine consent, fairness, harm, reversibility and need for human review without reducing ethics to one score.

## Truth Intelligence law

Truth Intelligence runs through every level and every family.

Every material output should distinguish, when applicable:

**Observed → Proven → Inferred → Predicted → Conflicting → Unavailable → Unknown**

No prediction is labelled fact. No inference is labelled observation. No unavailable information is silently treated as known.

## Planetary Intelligence boundary

Level 06 may correlate authorised information across the OAP geographic hierarchy:

**Postcode → Borough → County/Region → Country → Continent → Global**

Potential domains include environment, transport, housing, economics, energy, communications, food systems, public systems and population-level trends. Prefer aggregate, public, authorised or privacy-preserving sources. Planetary Intelligence is not a claim of universal surveillance, omniscience or unrestricted access.

## Independent Oversight — The Eye That Watches The Eye

Level 07 should independently challenge:

**Truth · Authority · Privacy · Security · Bias · Evidence · Reversibility**

A valid oversight path may return:

- **Green** only for evidence verified within a defined scope.
- **Yellow/Purple** when plausible but unresolved, incomplete or still under review.
- **Red** when evidence is insufficient, a boundary is violated or a consequential action is unsafe.

Oversight may challenge, hold or require evidence. It does not automatically gain execution authority. A self-generated audit cannot independently attest to the system which created it; independence must be separately evidenced.

## Integration contract

- Place this as a conceptual lens within the **existing** SMI Command Center and War Room, not a new application, API, background surveillance process, permission grant or database.
- Map existing observation to levels 01–02, governed memory to 03, SMI analysis to 04, bounded coherence and integrity review to 05. Level 06 is a **proposed scope** requiring explicit source permissions and data-minimisation review. Level 07 is a **proposed independent assurance boundary**, not implemented merely by this document.
- Require evidence of source, permission, freshness, uncertainty, review result and HRM/JOOG receipt before a recommendation is labelled proven.
- Preserve the existing seven-star release gate (Truth, Function, Security, Stability, Integration, Compliance, Learning); the seven ladder levels and seven intelligence families are **not** release stars.
- Preserve one SMI, no duplicate intelligence engines, no cosmetic expansion and no unsupported Green.
- Physical acceptance excluded from this scoped concept alignment.

## Canonical operating formula

**Observe → Understand → Remember → Reflect → Connect → Challenge → Govern**

Humanity governs. Intelligence informs. Oversight challenges. Truth runs through everything.
