Status: **governed policy proposal**, not a deployed autonomous agent, performance measurement service, or permission grant.

## Separate the three scores

- **Capability fit (1–7★):** defined specialist usefulness for a specific mission; explicitly labelled *provisional assessment*.
- **Verified performance (1–7★ or UNRATED):** only assign when attributable execution, exact-artifact tests, outcomes, and audit receipts exist. No receipts = UNRATED, not 7★ or 0★.
- **Mission priority (1–7★):** temporary task assignment based on fit, risk, availability and overlap. Priority is not permission.

A star rating is never a percentage of completion or proof of production readiness. Scores may not be averaged to override a failed mandatory gate.

## Seven levels and decisions

| Stars | Classification | Governance action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 7 | Legendary | Proven exceptional specialist within its assigned domain; review evidence before renewal |
| 6 | Exceptional | High-impact specialist; eligible for promotion with receipts |
| 5 | Excellent | Strong documented delivery |
| 4 | Strong | Maintain bounded responsibilities |
| 3 | Developing | Targeted improvement plan |
| 2 | Limited | Reduce mission priority, preserve mandatory controls |
| 1 | Critical review | Restrict discretionary assignments and require human review |

**0★ / SUSPENDED** is a separate, human-governed restriction after documented serious findings; it is not a normal performance rating. Safety containment may fail closed pending review.

## Evidence-based promotion, downgrade and recovery

Record for each material decision: agent/capability ID, family, mission, distinct responsibility, current and proposed ratings (by score type), evidence links, exact commit/artifact, test or outcome, overlap assessment, risk, dissent, reviewer, timestamp, and HRM receipt. Promotion requires attributable repeated verified outcomes. Downgrade requires documented failure, unnecessary duplication, or changed mission fit; lack of evidence yields UNRATED for performance. Recovery requires fresh independent proof and a recorded review. No automatic score change grants, transfers or revokes production permissions.
