Private activation

Use a unique password between 12 and 128 characters. A long password or passphrase is best; spaces, numbers and symbols are allowed. Do not use a short PIN or a password used elsewhere.

Forgot password is unavailable during first setup because no Founder password exists yet. Activation creates it.

The password is sent once to the managed identity service. OAP does not store it, display it, or place it in deployment configuration.
